** Genre:** Romance/Drama

# Information about the movie 
* This story revolves around a young couple who are in a long-term relationship and have a huge dream of building their future together.
* As their story goes on, it encounters a lot of ups and downs in the story
* It portrays the struggle of pursuing dreams due to uncertain scenarios since life is full of uncertainties due to imbalance foundation.
* It shows how love changes one's view over time as the foundation of their relationship become the root of their separation which proves that life doesn't always go according to our plan.
* The movie also shows how hard it can be when you have different dreams to pursue. 
* Being away from each other leads them to success and it explores the individual growth of the characters as the movie goes on.
* All in all, this movie revolves around the real-life depiction of love challenges that engraved a good lesson to the audience.
